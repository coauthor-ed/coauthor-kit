# Course shapes

The menu for Stage 1b plans in an interactive run (`briefs/reference/interactive.md`). A shape is a course's spine: the order of ideas and what carries the learner from lesson to lesson. `docs/pedagogy.md` holds in every shape. Name one main shape; 3 or 4 may be mixed with 2.

| # | Shape | Spine | Looks | Cost and risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Source order | The source's own order. Each lesson still opens on a question. | any | Cheapest. Matches the exam and the textbook. Risk: dull. |
| 2 | Puzzle first | Each lesson opens on a puzzle, a surprising number or a wrong guess the lesson then resolves. Order chosen for understanding. | any | Low. Today's default. Risk: the order differs from the textbook's. |
| 3 | Case spine | One case, dataset or investigation runs through every lesson. Each lesson adds a clue or a tool; the last lesson closes the case. E.g. an outbreak traced week by week; a bridge designed span by span; a city's water supply; a family budget over a year; a court case. | any (classic lessons, no cast) | Medium: shared scenes are reused. Risk: forcing ideas that do not fit the case. Real case data must be cited, licensed and de-identified. |
| 4 | Decision-led | The learner takes a role (health officer, engineer, editor, farmer) and makes a choice each lesson; the interactive shows what follows. | any | High: one decision scene per lesson. The engine has no branching lessons: choices live inside scenes and guess-first questions. A choice that must change later slides is `[needs new work]`. |
| 5 | Two parts | Part 1 builds intuition in plain words and puzzles. Part 2 makes it exact: notation, definitions, proofs. A multi-part bundle (START-HERE section 0). | any | Medium; more lessons. Each idea belongs to exactly one part; Part 2 may quiz Part 1, never re-teach it. Risk: textbook order differs. |
| 6 | Story mode | A cast and comic panels carry the course. | quill only | High writing cost. Risk: tone may not suit adults. |
| 7 | Game-led | Each lesson is built around a game whose win rule is the idea; arcade practice for recall. | quill only | Medium to high. Every game must clear tie level L2 (`docs/mini-games.md`). |

Playful under `plain`: shapes 2-5 work. The fun comes from the puzzle, the case, the decision and the data, not from characters, scores or arcade games (plain card).

Pick by the source and the learner:
- An exam-bound course with fixed order: 1 or 2.
- A subject with real cases or data (health, economics, environment, law, engineering): 3 or 4.
- A notation-heavy subject that learners find dry: 5, or 2.
- Young learners and a playful author: 6 or 7 (look quill).
