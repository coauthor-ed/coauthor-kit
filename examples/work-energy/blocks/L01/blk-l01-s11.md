# Challenge: Load Lifter

<!-- STEP -->
## How the game on the next slide works

On the next slide you play a game called **Load Lifter**. These are its rules.

A target work value is shown at the top of the game panel. Choose the mass on the hook, choose how high to lift it, and choose the direction of the pull. Then press **Lift**.

- If you overshoot the target, the attempt is wasted.
- If you pull straight across the lift, the work reads **zero** every time, and zero never matches a target.
- To hit the target, the force and the distance must multiply to exactly the right number.

<!-- STEP -->
## Think before you pull

In the game, the sign also changes. Lifting up against the weight gives positive work. Lowering the mass gives negative work, and the target does not accept a negative value.

**The only way to win is to use $F \times s$.**
