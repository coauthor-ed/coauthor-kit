# The Sack on the Shelf

<!-- STEP -->
## The lifting is over

The sack is on the shelf. Nobody is touching it. The push has stopped and the lifting is finished.

Yet the panel next to the sack still shows the work value. It has not faded or vanished.

<!-- STEP -->
## So what did the work do?

Work is a **transfer**. It happens while a force moves something, and then it is finished. What it leaves behind is something the sack can **keep**, even when it is perfectly still.

**The work was the transfer. What the sack now has is stored energy.**

What is this stored energy, and what can it do? That is the next question.
