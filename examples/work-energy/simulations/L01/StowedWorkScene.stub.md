# StowedWorkScene - simulation stub
Save path: simulations/L01/StowedWorkScene.js
Attached by: blk-l01-s13 (hook)
Beats: 2
Base class: DomSimulation (a shelf, a sack and two labelled panels with lit values - a still life with readouts, nothing to draw and nothing to animate)
Hero: A sack standing on a shelf with a lit work value on a panel beside it. At the other side of the scene stands the same brick wall from the wall scene, unmoved, with its own panel reading 0.
Dials: shelf height 1-4 m; a tap target on the sack panel that brightens its value and holds it lit.
Run shows: none. Tapping the sack panel lights the number and it stays lit. The wall panel stays at 0 and never changes.
Note card: the panel shows a bare number and its unit and is deliberately given no name. Naming what it is is the next lesson job, not this scene.
Must get right: the sack panel is lit and unchanged after the lift is over - it does not fade, drain, empty or count down; the wall beside it still reads zero; nothing anywhere on screen converts one value into another.
Not yet taught: the word energy, and any name at all for what the lit number is - the panel carries a unit and nothing else. No "capacity to do work", no energy story, no machines, no power.
Reuse: none.

## Per beat
- beat 0: the shelf, the sack with its panel already lit, and the wall still standing at zero. Tapping the panel re-brightens it; it never dims on its own.
- beat 1: unchanged. This beat asks the closing question in words; the scene answer is the pair of panels sitting there, one lit and one at zero, and it must not resolve anything further.

## Verbatim slide markdown
### blk-l01-s13
```markdown
# The Sack on the Shelf

<!-- STEP -->
## The lifting is over

The sack is on the shelf. Nobody is touching it. The push has stopped and the lifting is finished.

Yet the panel next to the sack still shows the work value. It has not faded or vanished.

<!-- STEP -->
## So what did the work do?

Work is a **transfer**. It happens while a force moves something, and then it is finished. What it leaves behind is something the sack can **keep**, even when it is perfectly still.

**The work was the transfer. What the sack now has is stored energy.**

What is this stored energy, and what can it do? That is the next question.
```
