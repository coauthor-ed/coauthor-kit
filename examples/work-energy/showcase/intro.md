## What this is

You push a wall for ten minutes and your arms hurt. Did you do any work? In physics, no. 

This first lesson of the chapter on work and energy shows why. The course content follows the CBSE syllabus for Class 9.

## What your child does

- Pushes, carries and lifts things in small simulations, and watches the work change as they turn a dial.
- Learns that work is force multiplied by the distance moved along the force.
- Finds out when work is positive, negative or zero.
- Reads work off the area under a force and distance graph.

## Before you start

Your child should know what a force is and what a newton is. Every slide has questions with hints, and the lesson fits in one sitting.
