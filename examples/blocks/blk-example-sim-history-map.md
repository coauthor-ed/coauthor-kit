# The Leap to Urban Splendour

## An Empty-Looking Map

Look at a map of the ancient Sarasvatī and Indus river valleys around 7000 BCE.

More than 1,500 settlements would eventually rise here, spread across 1.5 million square kilometres - but on this map, almost nothing shows yet.

**A civilisation that would one day cover a landmass this size started here, almost invisibly.**
