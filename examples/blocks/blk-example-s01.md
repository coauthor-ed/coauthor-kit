# Why can't one specialised tissue move an arm?

## Four Tissues, No Movement

The growing organism now has protective tissue, supporting tissue, muscle, and communication tissue.

Each one solves its own problem. Yet none of them, alone, can move anything.

## The Missing Piece

Place your hand flat on a desk. Now lift a heavy school bag off the floor beside you.

The arm rises smoothly. It doesn't collapse, twist limp, or stay still.

**If every tissue already does its own job, why does none of them produce this simple action alone? Something is still missing.**
