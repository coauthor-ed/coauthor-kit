# Splitting a Triangle in Half

<!-- STEP id="take-any-triangle" -->
Take any triangle $ABC$ with base $b$ and height $h$.

Make an identical copy. Flip it upside down about the midpoint of side $AC$, and slide it into place.

<!-- STEP id="the-two-congruent" -->
The two triangles now fit together perfectly.

**They form a parallelogram with the same base $b$ and the same height $h$.**

<!-- STEP id="because-the-two" -->
The two triangles are congruent - same shape, same size. So they have equal area.

<!-- STEP id="text-area-of" -->
Each triangle is exactly half of that parallelogram:

$$\text{Area of Triangle} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$$
