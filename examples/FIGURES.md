## Fig 3.1 (p.4) - Longitudinal section of a root tip

- Used by: L01 s03
- Inspected: rasterized + viewed directly
- Description: a vertical cross-section, root cap at the bottom (dome-shaped, darker
  shading), meristematic zone directly above it as a narrow band of small
  densely-packed cells, elongation zone above that with visibly longer/looser
  cells, maturation zone at the top where root hairs project outward from the
  surface. Four zones stacked bottom-to-top, no side-by-side elements.
- Numbers/values: none (purely structural diagram, no measurements labeled).

## Fig 3.3 (p.6) - Rate of cell division vs. distance from root tip

- Used by: L01 s05
- Inspected: rasterized + viewed directly
- Description: a line graph, x-axis "distance from root tip (mm)" 0-10, y-axis
  "relative rate of cell division" 0-100%. Curve peaks near x=1-2mm (~90%), falls
  sharply to near 0% by x=5mm, flat afterward - this is the meristematic zone's
  location relative to the tip, load-bearing for the s05 predict/explain pair.
- Numbers/values: peak ~90% at 1-2mm, drops below 10% by x=5mm - used for the s05
  question's plausible-distractor ranges, not restated verbatim in lesson prose.
